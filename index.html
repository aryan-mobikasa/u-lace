The Fit Finder

Not sure which size to buy? That's exactly why we built this.

Most no-tie laces are one-size-fits-all — which really means they fit nobody well. We measured thousands of sneakers across dozens of brands and discovered the key measurement: the eyelet span; the distance between adjacent eyelets across the top of the shoe. Our fit finder uses this measurement to give you the unique uLace size needed for each sneaker you wish to convert into hassle-free slip-ons.
