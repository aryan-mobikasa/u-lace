Eyelet-span is the gap between two eyelets across the top of your sneaker. Measure it once, pick the size whose range it falls in. Classic fits 2.25″ to 4.25″.

Details

What’s in the box−
6 modular elastic lace segments and a photo instruction sheet. Most sneakers have 5–7 eyelet pairs per shoe, so one pair of sneakers needs 2 packs.
Materials+
Truly-woven, yarn-dyed polyester around uLace’s own extruded lycra filament. That combination is what gives the laced-feel fit — snug, with give — instead of the stiff, one-size feel of silicone laces.

Will they work on my sneaker?+
Classic fits sneakers with an eyelet-span of 2.25″–4.25″ — the distance between two eyelets across the top of the shoe. Modular uLaces work on sneakers with eyelets; for loops or hooks, choose ProFormance. Not sure? Take the 30-second fit quiz.
